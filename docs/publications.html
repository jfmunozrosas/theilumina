Modified

November 16, 2026

Target journals

The main dissemination channel is publication in leading journals (JCR Q1/Q2 or ABS 3/4/4*). Methodological results (WP1–WP3) target journals such as Journal of Econometrics, Sociological Methods & Research, Social Indicators Research, Journal of Economic Inequality and Computational Statistics & Data Analysis. The R package (WP4) targets the Journal of Statistical Software, and the applications (WP5) target journals in Water Resources, Tourism, Management and Business.

Project publications

To be completed as papers are submitted and published.

Software

  • theil: Theil indices, bias corrections, variances and confidence intervals. R package with a C++ core. Forthcoming on CRAN.
  • giniVarCI: Gini indices, variances and confidence intervals for finite and infinite populations. https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=giniVarCI
  • samplingVarEst: Sampling variance estimation. https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=samplingVarEst
