Contact and collaboration

For researchers, institutions and organisations interested in collaborating with THEILUMINA

Modified

November 16, 2026

We welcome contact from external researchers and organisations, in particular in the transfer areas of the project:

Water
Water economics, governance and policy; access to water and sanitation; water consumption, reuse and the circular water economy
Tourism
Tourism economics, competitiveness and sustainability; seasonality; tourism and regional development; spatial and spatio-temporal analysis
Firms
Servitization, digital transformation and AI adoption; innovation; international business, alliances and acquisitions
Poverty and inequality measurement
Inequality and poverty indicators; well-being and quality of life; decomposition of inequality by groups, regions and countries
Methods and software
Bias correction, jackknife inference, computational efficiency and the R package theil

const CONTACT_EMAIL = "jfmunoz@ugr.es";
function theiluminaContact(e) {
  e.preventDefault();
  const v = id => document.getElementById(id).value.trim();
  const subject = `[THEILUMINA] Collaboration: ${v("cf-area")}`;
  const body = `Name: ${v("cf-name")}\nInstitution: ${v("cf-inst")}\nArea of interest: ${v("cf-area")}\nType of collaboration: ${v("cf-type")}\n\n${v("cf-msg")}`;
  window.location.href = `mailto:${CONTACT_EMAIL}?subject=${encodeURIComponent(subject)}&body=${encodeURIComponent(body)}`;
  return false;
}
</script>
